# Demo notes

[Download the music video, voice-over and thumbnail](https://github.com/KaushikSiva/Dropbeat/releases/tag/v0.1.0).

The opening prompt describes an original rapper in San Francisco, rapping about YC founders. Garry Tan, Shakira, tiger and ocean are introduced by manually dragging their pictures in that order. The prompt does not introduce them in advance.

The product demo includes an AI-generated Indian-English narration, actual Duo Simulator recording, live instrumental plus reactive spoken rap, and real playback of the finished file in the native app. Connection and final-generation waits are shortened and labelled.

The live model did not reliably retain likenesses or complete every visual change within the minute. The final music video therefore adds a supplied portrait, a separately generated and animated trio shot, and earlier generated tiger/ocean shots. These are editorial inserts, not claimed to have appeared immediately in the live capture. The final vocal performance is original; the pictured people’s voices are not cloned.

The sample music video is a 60-second, 3840 × 2160 export at 30 fps with stereo AAC audio and upscaled generated footage. It does not imply endorsement by the people or organizations depicted.

The repository’s small bundled example is a different ocean/tiger/city take, compressed for quick cloning and offline playback. The full 4K demo files are release assets rather than Git blobs.

## Record your own walkthrough

Use your actual app capture for the presentation. [Download the Indian-English voice-over](https://github.com/KaushikSiva/Dropbeat/releases/download/v0.1.0/DropBeat-Voiceover.wav) and play it over an introduction before you enter the prompt.

### Opening prompt

> Create a one-minute, high-energy English rap music video. An original rapper performs on a San Francisco waterfront promenade, with the Golden Gate Bridge behind him in warm morning light. The song celebrates YC founders: bold ideas, building fast, learning from failure, and making something people want. Use punchy drums, deep bass, clever original rhymes, and a catchy “build, launch, repeat” hook. Keep the rapper visible as the camera moves smoothly around him.

Preload your pictures. Once the video is running, introduce Garry, then Shakira, then tiger, then ocean by dropping each one onto the video. Wait for each acceptance message. Leave those subjects out of the opening prompt. Keep any waiting-time edits visible to viewers and distinguish the live result from the finished edit.

### Voice-over script

> Hello! This is DropBeat. You can direct a music video while it’s being made. I’m starting with a rap about YC, right here in San Francisco. Then I just drag in a picture—and the scene starts changing, with the next rap lines following along. Let’s bring in Garry, then Shakira… add a tiger… and take the whole thing out onto the ocean. Watch what happens.
